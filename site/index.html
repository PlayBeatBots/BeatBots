FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

ALL YOU NEED TO KNOW ABOUT BEATBOTS

What network is BeatBots built on? ▼

BeatBots is built natively on Solana to ensure sub-second asynchronous battle validations, micro-cent minting and transaction fees, and instant wallet signatures.

Is the game pay-to-win? ▼

No. Rhythmic accuracy, timing, and combo streaks determine match victory. NFTs grant cosmetic variations, status badges, sound pack expansions, and scrap multipliers, but never raw score advantages in competitive brackets.

Can I export and use the beats I make in the 16-step sequencer? ▼

Yes! Patterns you program in the 16-Step Beat Maker synthesize live in-browser using the Web Audio API. You can play, randomize, and test them live or take them straight into the rhythm duel engine.
